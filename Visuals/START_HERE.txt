AVALON VISUALS
DayZ Steam 1.30 | GTX 1660 Ti 6 GB | i9-9900K | 32 GB RAM

PLAY
Use Client > Play Avalon Enhanced for the Avalon Visuals quality preset
and daylight color preview in one click. Every Enhanced launch selects
quality mode: farther views, more object detail, and cleaner edges.
Azimuth signs the preview and loads it on both server and client.
If Avalon is running without it, Azimuth waits for the server to be empty
before restarting it and joining. The graphics mode and preview choice
are saved for later launches.

Close DayZ and the DayZ Launcher, then click Play Avalon in Azimuth.
Azimuth prepares the server, applies the chosen native graphics preset
to your saved client profile, and joins with BattlEye.

Avalon Visuals requests 5,000 m terrain and 3,000 m object visibility.
It raises object detail, texture detail, shadows, and filtering, with
hardware anti-aliasing and foliage smoothing. Higher saved detail settings
are preserved. Shadows extend to at least 200 m where the profile supports
them. These quality settings use more GPU and CPU and can lower FPS.

The preset uses the installed map's models and textures; it does not
replace those assets. Their detail still limits how the landscape looks.
The server and weather can further limit actual visibility in game.

Choose Apply FPS preset in Client for lower visibility limits of 1,600 m
and 1,000 m. That choice is saved for ordinary Play Avalon launches;
Play Avalon Enhanced switches back to quality mode. Use Restore graphics
to restore the original managed settings and keep your own in-game choices
on later ordinary launches. Your resolution and display mode are still
set in DayZ's Video menu.

DAYZ LAUNCHER
For a manual join, close the game and launcher, click Apply Avalon
Visuals in Azimuth, then join normally with that same DayZ profile. Use
Client > Choose profile if you want to select a different graphics file.
Azimuth remembers your choice for later launches.

RESHADE COMPATIBILITY
ReShade's standard installer blocks DayZ_x64.exe. The old Avalon Looks
button copied only preset files. Play Avalon uses native graphics.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crosire/reshade-shaders/list/Compatibility.ini

EXPERIMENTAL DAYLIGHT COLOR
Stop Avalon and close DayZ. In Client, click Enable color preview and then
Play Avalon. Azimuth builds the local mod, signs it with installed DayZ
Tools, and loads it with both server and client. The preview adds subtle
warmth and contrast outdoors during Avalon daylight, from 08:00 to 16:59.
The grade is designed to pause for survival and camera effects. Live
appearance and compatibility with the installed mod list need validation.
To return to standard color, stop Avalon, click Disable color preview,
then click Play Avalon. This setting does not reset the world.
