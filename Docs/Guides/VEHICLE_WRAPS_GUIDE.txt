AZIMUTH VEHICLE WRAPS AND GO-KART SHOWCASE

INSTALL AND PLAY
Follow Docs/START_HERE.md for the complete application ZIP. Stop the old server
and make a backup before replacing application files. Keep Worlds, Config, Mods
and Backups. Existing characters, vehicles and world saves do not need a reset.

OPTIONAL GO-KART SHOWCASE
In Skins / Camos select your finishes, enable vehicle wraps, set Chance to 100%
and check "Spawn one test go-kart beside me". Save with the server stopped,
then Play Avalon. Stand outdoors on clear, fairly flat ground for ten seconds.
The placement search retries while connected and announces successful delivery.
The MBM Go-Kart uses its actual battery, spark plug and four wheels; its optional
flashlight is omitted because the publisher documents a headlight-key conflict.

The kart and original Gunter have separate durable one-time receipts. Restarting,
reconnecting, toggling the checkbox or installing this repair does not repeat
completed delivery. Missing vehicle/part classes do not consume the request.
For normal play, disable the showcase and choose your preferred wrap chance.

CHANCE AND SELECTED FINISHES
The 30-finish collection is shared across compatible weapons, weapon parts,
helmets, clothing, backpacks and vehicles. Checked finishes control new random
choices; required assets for previously installed fixed-skin guns are retained.
Vehicle chance is independent of weapon/gear chance. Saved settings are retained.
100% applies to eligible vehicles, not every model or every surface. Results
remain stable with unchanged settings; changing the chosen finishes or chance
can change the result after a restart.

Existing vehicles receive wraps when loaded. Mapped fitted panels follow their
parent's finish and restore their original texture on detach. Failed writes
retain the information needed for bounded retries. An unrelated external paint
change is preserved. Wraps do not change handling, fuel, cargo or spawn rates.
Native damage materials and protected glass, lights and tyres stay intact.

CURRENT COVERAGE
738 public variants: 703 with an exterior mapping, 33 stock-only and 2 with
fitted panels only. "Exterior mapping" means at least one eligible section;
it does not promise whole-body coverage or native visual verification.
VEHICLE_VARIANTS.csv contains the exact classes and VEHICLE_WRAP_COVERAGE.txt
explains partial coverage and the stock-model list.

The Airship wraps its banners, not its main hull; the galleon maps sails.
Vodnik and Gaz69 have partial exterior coverage. Humvee Multi-shader door
exteriors still need material-specific wrapping; texture readback alone is
not proof that their camo is visible. KA60/Mi171 mixed distant-LOD sections
are protected. Models without an independent exterior section remain stock.

WORKSHOP AND AUTOMATIC PLACEMENT
Supported downloaded packs are added to server and client together on the next
stopped-server start, including the newly decoded LMs Planes pack. Steam pending
updates and required dependencies are respected. A previously used pack missing
from a save blocks launch instead of silently loading without its vehicle classes.
No third-party PBO is rewritten.

Large ships, Airship and additional aircraft use suitable dedicated positions
or automatic Avalon terrain/clearance survey results. Play may wait up to two
minutes and perform one clean setup backup/restart. If discovery takes longer,
existing transport stays available and accepted new positions import later.
No manual vehicle-by-vehicle test round is required for normal setup.

EVIDENCE AND RECEIPTS
Your Gunter screenshot confirms that car's visible wrap. Other coverage comes
from supplied configuration/model/material analysis and executable source tests.
Native DayZ compilation, physics and visual testing were not run here.

Profiles/AzimuthSkins/vehicle_wrap_coverage.json records server choices and
coverage observations; actual client per-slot state is checked separately.
Profiles/AzimuthSkins/vehicle_gokart_showcase_state.json is the kart receipt.
The original vehicle_showcase_state.json Gunter receipt is preserved.
