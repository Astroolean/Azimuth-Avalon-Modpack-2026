AZIMUTH WEAPON AND CLOTHING CAMOS

Use Skins / Camos to select from the same 30 finishes. Random skins on weapons
and gear controls the chance for each compatible item independently. The saved
percentage also applies to compatible helmets, clothing and backpacks; vehicles
keep their separate wrap percentage. Only checked finishes enter new random
choices. Assets needed by previously installed fixed-skin guns remain available.

Firearms, magazines, stocks, handguards, suppressors, bayonets and compatible
melee-capable items retain the same underlying item classes and gameplay.
Clothing is now identified separately from melee weapons. Named ground, male
and female clothing paint selections are handled where the model exposes them.
Faces, optic selections, glass/visor selections and known alpha/cutout textures
are not paint targets. Some models use combined atlases or expose no compatible
paint selection; those need their own model-specific treatment.

The application repair reads each current texture instead of treating a cached
finish ID as success. Failed or delayed writes get bounded retries. Native
texture resets can be repaired after synchronization, health and inventory
changes. Unrelated external repaints and original damage materials are retained.
Your existing saved percentages and selected finishes are unchanged.

To apply changes: stop Avalon normally, close DayZ, save the selection, then
start through Azimuth. Changing choices can change an existing random finish;
with unchanged choices it is derived from the item's persistent ID. No save
stream fields or gameplay stats are added.

Fixed older Azimuth M4A1/AKM variants are different from random normal-class
items. Azimuth retains their previously installed classes and assets when you
uncheck finishes, including when all finishes are off. Existing test chests also
remain supported. New random choices still use only your current selection.

Remove test kit cleans up reachable eligible test items. If recorded items have
gone away or are out of reach, cleanup is deferred without discarding their
records or blocking your finish choices. The page explains this state. Use
Remove test kit again if those items become reachable. Reachable items with
protected added gear remain pending until that gear is removed safely.

The asset audit checks all 30 textures/previews and 60 fixed weapon variants.
The regression audit checks renderer behavior with extracted production methods.
Those checks do not replace native DayZ compilation or visual gameplay results.
