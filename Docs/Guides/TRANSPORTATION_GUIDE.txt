AZIMUTH EXPANDED TRANSPORTATION
Current guide | November 14, 2026

WHAT THIS UPDATE DOES
Enables the disabled Olga, Gunter and M3S truck events. Adds seven native DayZ
1.30 motorcycle variants plus Expansion helicopters, a gyrocopter, extra cars,
an amphibious vehicle, buses, tractors and boats. Adds installed-publisher-data
setup for rideable horses and the An-2 airplane. No live save is bundled or wiped.
Previously disabled road events are enabled using their complete effective
definitions, preserving their original values and children.
All previous 30 skins, I inspection, P freecam, graphics and solo-loot fixes remain.

INSTALL
Follow Docs/START_HERE.md. Before upgrading the older manager, stop Avalon,
use Backups > Back up now and confirm its archive, then close DayZ and Azimuth.
Extract the complete Azimuth folder; keep Worlds, Config, Mods and Backups. Update DayZ
and DayZ Server to the same current build, then use START_SERVER.bat > Play Avalon.
The repaired manager checks matching builds and required downloads. No reinstall,
new character, world wipe, or manual round of vehicle tests is required.

ADDITIONAL DOWNLOADED PACKS
The 14 supported additional packs contain 580 catalogued variants. They load
for both client and server when their downloads/dependencies are ready. Small
vehicles, road vehicles, trucks, boats and helicopters use bounded mixed pools
and literal installed part classes. Your existing one-time Gunter/go-kart
receipts remain intact; the showcase is optional and is not requeued by updating.
See VEHICLE_WRAP_COVERAGE.txt and VEHICLE_VARIANTS.csv for exact class coverage.
LMs Planes is integrated from the supplied full archive: 12 vehicle variants,
16 actual part classes, nine land aircraft and three seaplanes. Two authored
airfield event anchors are transferred to a bounded land-plane event (target
one). Seaplanes use the large-water survey route; existing An-2 placement stays.
Publisher scripts and vehicle physics remain installed from Workshop.

AUTOMATIC PLACEMENT
The server-only survey checks candidate Avalon sites for 15 large vessels, one
Airship and separate additional-helicopter placements. It creates/removes no
vehicles. Only complete matching reports provide new sites. Large ships need
deep clear water; aircraft need level, clear ground. Existing dedicated positions
are preserved, and new additional-helicopter sites do not reuse Expansion's six
allocated pad anchors. Unsafe fallback positions are not guessed.

Play Avalon displays Checking Avalon vehicle placement sites for up to two
minutes. One clean backup/restart can apply accepted sites together with first-
run fishing setup before joining. If discovery continues, play proceeds using
existing transport; accepted new sites are imported on a later start. The live
survey was not executed in this offline repair environment. No manual coordinate
survey or per-model test assignment is required.

Airship assembly follows the publisher's ready-on-spawn behavior. AirshipRemote
receives an ordinary floor-loot route where verified; the admin shuttle remote
stays excluded. The wrap covers the Airship banners and galleon sails, not their
entire hulls. Lack of a suitable surveyed site remains an explicit spawn limit.

VEHICLES AND SOURCES
Existing: ADA/Sarka, Humvee, four RZR variants, pedal bicycles and motorboats.
Enabled: Olga, Gunter and M3S truck colour variants, with matching spare wheels.
Motorcycles: Jana 50 and Bitrak 682, seven colour variants in total.
Expansion: MH6 Little Bird, UH1H, Merlin, gyrocopter; UAZ, Land Rover, Vodnik,
bus and tractor; Zodiac and RHIB. Its 57 configured variants include paint and
body variations, not 57 unrelated vehicle models.

Horse: imports literal classes, AI behavior and equipment rules from the
installed DayZ Horse setup files. Uses three existing Avalon cattle grazing
areas, including their rest/water zones. Saddles and bridles receive ordinary
Farm/Village supply routes. Riding needs both; apples can lure a horse.

An-2: imports the literal base-aircraft class and required attachments from the
installed publisher ServerFiles. One existing airfield-area crash-event position
is transferred to its own plane event so that crash site cannot occupy the same
point. Optional repaint classes are not invented or assumed installed.

HORSE AND AIRPLANE DATA CHECK
The Horse importer has since been checked against your supplied publisher
XML and horse class definitions. It repairs the known malformed comment in its
parsing copy and uses Avalon locations; installed Workshop files stay unchanged.
An-2 validation now recognizes its known cargo/passenger/storage door classes
and rejects incompatible wheel attachments. Supplied configuration and fixture
checks do not establish native horse behavior, flight or collision results.
If required setup data is missing or unsupported, the manager says NEEDS SETUP
and does not claim that category is spawning. Other transport can still run.
Keep the original Workshop Extras/ServerFiles/manual folders alongside Addons
and Keys. Verify the Workshop download if a required folder is missing.
Use the control panel's Loot audit or Tools/VERIFY_PLAYTHROUGH.bat after a normal stop
to see outstanding setup notices. An-2 also writes its status to
Worlds/avalon/Profiles/Azimuth/Transport Plane Audit.json.

PARTS AND SPAWN BEHAVIOR
Motorcycles have both wheels fitted; spark plugs use a 75% attachment chance.
Expansion engine parts and wheels use complete attachment rules, with optional
body panels retaining their authored chances. Spare wheels, engine components,
hydraulic fluid, hoses and related supplies have ordinary loot routes.
Fuel and replacement supplies are still scavenged. Vehicles are not magically
refuelled, teleported to you or handed out as another test kit.

The disabled road lists included unverified legacy locations. New road vehicles
use exact positions from Avalon's active ADA/Sarka, Humvee and RZR event pools.
Motorcycles and each enabled road event have 28 candidate positions; bicycles
have up to 40, replacing the old five-position limit. Road categories share
candidate sites, so their target counts are not simultaneous parking capacity.
The Central Economy still controls occupancy, spacing and replenishment.

Expansion helicopters use six candidate anchors from eight authored Avalon helipads.
Two anchors fail the static origin-clearance check. The accepted anchors use
the pad's authored floor-height evidence. Rotor clearance, unlisted props,
terrain, slope, native collision, takeoff and landing are not certified by those
static checks. Additional helicopter variants use the separate survey above.
Boats reuse the creator's water-event positions. No Chernarus/Nasdara spawn
coordinates were pasted into Avalon. Existing loot refreshes over normal cycles.

ASSET AND APPEARANCE REPAIRS
The supplied full models and LM archive have been processed. Eight missing
Maharlika material references now use safe model-backed recovery: missing glass
material transitions retain the model material, and armor repaint selectors
use proven exterior materials. Three existing Cybertruck repairs remain.
The absent Techs ATV handlebars are omitted from new optional spawn recipes;
existing vehicles are not replaced or edited. No missing mesh was invented.

The 738-variant inventory has 703 exterior mappings, 33 stock-body variants and
two fitted-panel-only variants. Mapping does not mean every surface is wrapped.
The Airship hull remains stock; its isolated banners remain supported. Humvee
Multi-material door wrapping remains unresolved. See VEHICLE_WRAP_COVERAGE.txt
for exact limits. Collection remains optional diagnostics; no further export
is required for this release.

WHAT IS NOT ENABLED
HypeTrain currently documents ChernarusPlus, Enoch and Namalsk support. It has
no supported Avalon track setup in the checked material. Trains therefore need
a separate Avalon adaptation; enabling a train wreck event would not add one.
Unsupported classes and missing publisher assets are not replaced with guesses.
This is a broad supported transportation pack, not every Workshop vehicle mod.

HISTORICAL TRANSPORT UPDATE VERIFICATION
These are retained results from that update, not a native test of this release.
332 automated offline tests passed (265 existing regression checks plus
67 transportation/version/upgrade checks). Tests cover parent functionality,
positive parts/attachment routes, event imports, dependency deduplication,
version guards, pack upgrades, invalid input, rollback and complete preparation.
The actual manager pipeline ran twice with publisher fixtures and twice without
Horse/An-2 data. Both cases kept generated files byte-identical on repeat and
preserved save, skin-choice/chance and receipt fixtures plus original publisher
types/events/spawnables. Existing saves were not used or changed.

The no-Horse/no-plane fixture has 2,629 economy entries and 94 configured vehicle
variants across 20 active vehicle event families. The synthetic publisher-data
fixture has 2,636 entries, including its test horse/plane definitions. Those fixture
counts do not prove your installed Horse/An-2 classes, actual population or
time needed to find a vehicle.
Native compilation, signatures, physics, flight, performance and joining were
not run here. Signing is simulated only in offline tests; your original manager
still performs its normal signing/key checks on Windows.
