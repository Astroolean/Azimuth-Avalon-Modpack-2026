AZIMUTH VEHICLE WRAP COVERAGE
Updated 14 November 2026 from the supplied publisher configs and model files.

CURRENT MAPPING TOTALS
738 public vehicle variants, including 12 LMs Planes variants:
  703 have at least one exterior mapping.
   33 retain their stock appearance through this wrap system.
    2 have mapped fitted panels only: EXT_KA60 and Ford_F150Hoonitruck.

A mapping is an eligible exterior section, not a promise that every part of the
vehicle changes color. These are static configuration/model coverage totals,
not 738 native DayZ visual or driving tests. Your supplied screenshot confirms
the blue Gunter's visible wrap. Native DayZ testing was not performed here.

The new inspection parsed 145 supplied models, with no failed model LODs, and
checked 2,985 compiled selections against their actual face ranges. The repair
adds 24 precise config patches and safe exterior rules across all inspected
visual LODs. Original lights, glass, wheels, slot order and driving physics are
preserved. The full class-by-class list is VEHICLE_VARIANTS.csv.

WHAT CHANGED
Real exterior sections omitted from publisher configs are now exposed for the
Expansion gyrocopters, Vodnik rear module, RaG schoolbus/Gaz69, MMT bicycle,
MotorHome models and previously unpaintable white/base car variants. The Gaz69
hood now uses its actual compiled section. Existing saved vehicles can receive
wraps; no wipe, replacement vehicle or repeat showcase delivery is required.

LMs Planes is now read from its actual downloaded PBO and included in supported
pack setup. Nine LM variants have exterior mappings: DC-3, Spitfire, Z37,
three StuntPlane variants and three Tigermoth variants. Cessna180, Catalina and
Patty Wagon retain stock bodies because their models lack a safe independent
exterior section. Their transport integration is separate from cosmetic support.

IMPORTANT PARTIAL COVERAGE
- Airship: left/right banners only; main hull remains stock.
- Galleon: mapped sail selections; hull coverage is not claimed.
- Vodnik: independent rear exterior module; mixed cabin sections stay stock.
- Gaz69: safe back/fenders/hood; mixed interior/front/chassis sections stay stock.
- Humvee: texture mappings do not establish visual coverage of Multi-shader
  door exteriors. Material-specific door wrapping was not implemented; do not
  treat a successful texture readback as proof that those doors display camo.
- KA60: fitted mapped panels only. Its body camo section includes glass and
  interior surfaces at distant LODs, so it is not broadly painted.

STOCK-APPEARANCE VEHICLES
Ten RTrzr variants; four Expansion Zodiac variants; ExpansionLHD; RaG Willys;
Van CamperRV, HouseBoat, Sailboat, WoodenBoat, Catamaran, Yacht and five JetSki
variants; EXT MerlinHC3 and Mi171; Old_BikeArma; LM Cessna180, Catalina and
Patty Wagon. Exact class names appear in the CSV.

These models lack a safe independent exterior section, mix protected surfaces,
or use an alpha atlas requiring a different texture process. They remain
available for transportation. No arbitrary texture index or whole-model paint
fallback is used. Zodiac transparency is retained. Mi171's broad camo group
includes glass/interior at lower LODs and is intentionally left unchanged.

OPTIONAL ONE-TIME GO-KART
Stop the server. In Skins / Camos select finishes, enable vehicle wraps, set
Chance to 100%, and enable "Spawn one test go-kart beside me". Save, then Play
Avalon. Stand outdoors on clear, fairly flat ground for ten seconds. Placement
retries until a suitable nearby position is available; delivery is announced.

The MBM Go-Kart and existing Gunter use separate durable receipts. Rejoining,
restarting or installing this repair does not repeat a completed delivery.
The existing Gunter, saves, settings and receipt are retained. For normal play,
disable the showcase checkbox and choose your preferred chance, such as 20%.
100% means eligible vehicles receive a selected finish, not one spawn per login.

WORKSHOP AND SPAWNS
Supported packs load on server and client after their Steam downloads and
required dependencies are present. LMs Planes is included. Each pack retains
its own actual classes, parts and behavior. Cosmetic chance is independent
of the bounded transport population pools and available safe placements.

Large craft, airship and additional aircraft use suitable authored positions
or the automatic Avalon terrain/clearance survey. Play can wait up to two
minutes and perform one setup backup/restart to accept first-run survey data.
If discovery continues, existing transport remains available and accepted new
locations are imported later. Unsafe fallback positions are not used.
