AZIMUTH FULL AUDIT REPAIRS
14 November 2026 — current repair summary

This update repairs the confirmed source/configuration defects that can be
resolved from the supplied files. It does not claim flawless native DayZ behavior
or fabricate replacements for missing models and publisher assets. Earlier JSON
reports remain historical evidence; their test counts are not this release's total.

INSTALL WITHOUT RESETTING YOUR PLAYTHROUGH
Before updating an older manager: Stop, wait for DayZ Server to exit, use Backups
> Back up now and confirm the archive, then close DayZ and Azimuth. Older Stop
did not automatically back up. Extract the ZIP into the parent of your existing
Azimuth folder, merging Azimuth with Azimuth and replacing packaged files.
Keep Worlds, Config, Mods and Backups. Do not delete the installation first or
nest Azimuth inside Azimuth. Start with START_SERVER.bat and Play Avalon.
Your character, world, saved choices and one-time delivery receipts are retained.
No new round of manual model tests, respawn or world reset is required.

MANAGER, BACKUPS AND CLIENT
- Stop now pauses management, requests graceful shutdown and continues the final
  backup work. Only confirmed clean shutdown leads to a verified archive.
  Failure is reported as Backup delayed; shutdown is not force-killed to claim
  success. Repeated failed starts and unresponsive startup pause the retry loop.
- Shutdown evidence is tied to recorded launch provenance. Reopening cannot
  reuse a previous clean log to certify a newer run. Corrupt or incomplete
  archives do not become valid backups just because their filenames look recent.
- Restore selected validates the selected world's archive, requires a stopped
  and paused manager with the client closed, retains a pre-restore safety copy,
  and stages replacements with rollback/recovery. World/profile data, archived
  world settings and saved skin choices are restored together. Current connection
  settings remain, and the server stays stopped. A safety ZIP is also made when
  the current world has save files.
- Client/server executable builds must match, including the version recorded
  for the running server. A newer disk executable cannot hide an older process.
- Invalid or incomplete FPS graphics baselines are rejected before profile
  changes. Baseline extension and profile writes use transactional rollback.
- Workshop scan failure clears stale readiness and disables Enable pack. The
  13 integrated packs give download/restart guidance. UI completion errors no
  longer prevent later results or future timer polls from being processed.

SAVED COSMETICS AND PHOTO MODE
- Checked finishes control new random choices. Validated historical fixed-skin
  classes and recorded chest classes/assets remain available when deselected,
  including an empty selection. Retained classes have no new ordinary loot target.
- Missing or distant recorded test items retain their records but can defer
  cleanup without locking finish changes. Remove test kit can retry later;
  reachable items with protected gear remain pending until safe to remove.
- Photo mode hides the full native HUD root after the base update and restores
  its prior visibility on exit. Extracted-code tests cover the update ordering;
  native rendering was not executed here.

VEHICLES AND AUTOMATIC PLACEMENT
- An-2's known cargo/passenger/storage doors receive exact persistence rows;
  incompatible wheel/unknown attachment definitions are still rejected.
- Four shared native scalar fields are restored, with 79 source-backed local
  pins preserving publisher subclass behavior. Green Kamaz uses its twelve
  matching green panels. Three Cybertruck material paths now reference existing
  same-PBO glass-state assets; unrelated materials are not replaced with guesses.
- The supported additional catalog contains 568 variants across 13 packs.
  Airship uses its publisher-documented ready-on-spawn assembly and ordinary
  AirshipRemote supply where a verified floor route exists; admin remote excluded.
- Fifteen large vessels and Airship can enter bounded natural pools only with
  suitable dedicated existing sites or complete matching survey evidence.
  Additional helicopters use unused authored pads and separate surveyed sites;
  old managed positions are retained while new validated sites are pending.
- A server-only automatic Avalon survey checks depth, level ground, footprints
  and obstacle clearance without creating/removing vehicles. Play waits up to
  two minutes, then can perform one clean backup/restart to import accepted sites
  and fishing setup. If discovery continues, existing transport remains available;
  new accepted sites apply on a later start. No suitable site means no new spawn.
  Report validation is not a signed measurement certificate or a native result
  from this repair environment.

ECONOMY AND CHECKER
- All eight exact DayZ Bicycle variants use their validated retained attachment
  rules and 40 existing Avalon road sites. The precise Humvee min/max conflict
  is repaired without raising its authored nominal population.
- HipPack_Party, SVD_Wooden and PunchedCard receive verified ordinary usage
  routes while preserving their existing targets. Zero-target events and
  zero-capacity children no longer count as positive supply evidence.
- Missing BeeHive spatula/dipper/empty-pot companion rows can be recovered from
  actual installed publisher configuration only when concrete classes are
  unambiguous. Publisher XML is preserved. No class names were invented; the
  real BeeHive binary was unavailable here, so actual live discovery is unproved.
- The checker now validates event numbers, flags, modes and children; finite
  position pools; fixed vehicle sites; cargo/attachment/preset chances; duplicate
  roots; and required CE root/actions. Eight ambiguous map-prototype names
  remain warnings instead of receiving guessed precedence.

REMAINING SOURCE/MODEL LIMITS
- Of the 726 installed-config vehicle variants, 33 have no supported exterior
  mapping, eight have fitted-panel coverage only, and 19 need a safe nonempty
  opaque texture from the live model. Those are not fully wrapped bodies.
- Eight Maharlika material paths and TechsHandleBars' missing handlebar model
  have no proven supplied replacement. LMs Planes' export is unreadable and
  remains excluded. Horse-body wrapping and unsupported Avalon train systems
  are outside the current supported mechanism.
- The optional Workshop > Collect vehicle repair files button gathers relevant
  model/mod source evidence into one local ZIP and opens its folder. It does
  not upload files or include player saves/profiles. Use it only for the remaining
  source gaps; normal integrated pack setup does not require collection.
- Publisher script callback behavior, native geometry/clearance, exact appearance,
  vehicle handling, flight, multiplayer and spawn timing cannot be certified
  from configuration. Existing Gunter imagery proves only that observed car.
