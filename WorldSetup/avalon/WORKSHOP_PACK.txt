AZIMUTH | AVALON WORKSHOP PACK
DayZ Steam Stable 1.30.164289

PLAY
Click Play Avalon. Steam handles first-time subscriptions and updates.
When Steam opens a Workshop page, click Subscribe. Leave Azimuth open.
It checks local downloads, prepares Avalon, waits for the server, and
joins with the same mod order. Steam supplies the Workshop files.

IN GAME
M opens the map with your location. DayZ equips the Avalon Shore Kit on
first spawn and every respawn. It contains civilian clothes, a sling bag,
an apple, a cola, a bandage, a stone knife, matches and a green glow stick.
Your living character keeps their gear. The Workshop pack reduces stock
sprint stamina drain and loosens some placement checks for roofs and slopes.

Build a RaG Hunting Cabin or assemble a RaG BaseBuilding structure.
Building Fortifications adds barricades for existing buildings. RaG Base
Items adds workbenches and greenhouse kits. Solitude adds furniture and
storage. BeeHive adds beekeeping; some companion equipment may require
the publisher's economy setup. Hunter Life Machines still provides your
stove, coffee machine, and sewing machine.

Use an Airdrop Flare on open inland ground to call survival supplies.
Azimuth sets up flare calls; timed drops need verified Avalon landing
coordinates. A new drop can bring three infected. Existing custom drop
configurations retain their settings apart from a publisher logging fix.

Survivor Animations adds emotes. Bicycle, dog, and guitar files load with
the pack. Natural bike, kennel kit, guitar, and some furniture spawns depend
on usable publisher economy files or suitable Avalon vehicle locations.
Azimuth reports setup gaps in Server activity. Fishing loot is integrated
after the mod generates its files, normally during a one-time restart
before the first join. The sound system creates its configuration on first
boot. Custom sound zones use its in-game editor and Avalon locations.

FILES AND UPDATES
Steam keeps the subscribed items current. Azimuth reads the same installed
Workshop copies for client and server and checks for pending updates when
Steam lists them. The bundled Hunter Mods are local archives; Azimuth
checks their signatures and signs changed PBOs with DayZ Tools.

Avalon configuration and profiles live under Worlds/avalon. The mission's
azimuth_workshop folder contains each add-on's separate economy files.
Azimuth backs up existing Avalon saves before the first pack change.
Azimuth checks Solitude Storage against its Workshop signatures and
synchronizes its public key before Avalon starts.
Review any remaining publisher setup notices in Server activity.

LOOT AUDIT
Worlds > Avalon > Loot audit opens the audit after Azimuth prepares the
world. It lists counts, configured spawn routes, and mod setup gaps. It
covers ordinary loot, active event children, static wreck proxies, and
declared cargo and attachments. Azimuth applies a separate mission loot
overlay for restrained increases and checks real building categories and
tags where the terrain provides them. Regional tiers, craft recipes,
event placement and existing world storage keep their rules. Loot appears
as the running DayZ economy fills eligible points.

COMBINATION NOTES
Use Survivor Animations as the only player animation overhaul. Horse setup now
imports installed publisher data and uses Avalon grazing/rest/water territories.
Ambient Animals remains deferred until its Avalon territories are verified.
Zens Sleeping Mod requires a wipe and is excluded from the existing save.
BaseBuildingPlus is a separate alternative to RaG BaseBuilding.
