AVALON SETUP

Subscribe to Avalon in Steam Workshop and wait for the download:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3225634444

Also subscribe to Community Framework, required by Vanilla Painting:
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1559212036

Install free DayZ Tools in Steam for first-time signing of Hunter Mods:
https://store.steampowered.com/app/830640/DayZ_Tools/

Azimuth looks for the creator's mission in the downloaded mod folder. If the
mission is missing, look in Steam's workshop/content/221100/3225634444 folder
for the creator's mission files, or use the support link on the Workshop page.
For a .7z or .rar archive, extract it first. Place the extracted mission folder
here, keeping its original .avalon suffix and its init.c and db/types.xml.
Azimuth can also read a standard mission ZIP placed here. Restart the server
in Azimuth after adding missing files.
